Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for authors, attendees and sponsors.

Who should submit an abstract?

Experts from government, industry and academia working on the theory and applications of machine learning to challenging problems faced by Naval forces and their partners.

How long can my abstract be?

2,000 characters or less. Submit it through the official form at go.mil/naml2027sub by December 4, 2026.

Can my abstract contain CUI?

Abstracts must be unclassified and may contain up to Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI). Never submit classified information into the form.

I work for a company. Can I present?

Yes, but abstracts that read like a sales pitch or an overt promotion of a company, product or service will not be considered. For company visibility, see sponsorship.

Who can attend days 3 and 4?

Days 3–4 at NIWC Pacific are open only to appropriately cleared attendees who have a CAC or similar government ID, or are able to obtain a DBIDS credential. Days 1–2 in La Jolla are open to all participants.

Is there a fee?

Submitting an abstract is free. There will be a registration fee (TBD) to attend, waived for government civilian employees and military personnel.

When will I hear back?

Acceptance notifications will be sent to authors by mid-February 2027.

Who approves the release of my slides?

Presenting authors are responsible for obtaining the approvals needed to release their presentation materials before the workshop.

When does registration open?

Workshop registration will open soon. For registration and platform questions, email naml2027@underline.io.

Who do I contact with other questions?

The organizing committee at naml@us.navy.mil. Sponsorship: racurato@deloitte.com.

Abstracts due December 4, 2026

Unclassified, up to CUI, 2,000 characters or less.
