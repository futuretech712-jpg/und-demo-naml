About NAML

NAML is a forum for interdisciplinary experts from government, industry, and academia to discuss ongoing work on the theory and applications of machine learning to address some of the most challenging problems faced by our Naval forces and their partners.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for attendees and sponsors.

When does registration open?

Workshop registration will open soon on the Registration page.

Is there a registration fee?

There will be a registration fee (TBD) to attend NAML 2027. The fee is waived for government civilian employees and military personnel.

Who can attend days 3 and 4?

Days 3-4 at NIWC Pacific are open only to appropriately cleared attendees who have a CAC or similar government ID, or are able to obtain a DBIDS credential. Days 1-2 in La Jolla are open to all participants.

Where is the workshop?

Days 1-2 at the Hyatt Regency La Jolla, days 3-4 at NIWC Pacific in San Diego. Addresses are on the Venues page.

Who helps with registration?

Registration support: naml2027@underline.io.

Who do I contact with other questions?

The organizers at naml@us.navy.mil. Sponsorship: racurato@deloitte.com.
