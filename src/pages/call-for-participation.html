Abstract submission

  • Submit a short abstract (2,000 characters or less) for your presentation.
  • Abstracts must be unclassified and may contain up to Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI).
  • Abstracts will not be shared outside the government review team.
  • Presenting authors are responsible for obtaining the approvals needed to release their presentation materials before the workshop.
  • Abstracts that read like a sales pitch or an overt promotion of a company, product, or service will not be considered. For sponsorship, see Sponsors.

Do not submit classified information into the submission form.

How to submit

  1. Pick a format: short talk, lightning talk, poster or another format.
  2. Write an abstract of 2,000 characters or less. Unclassified, up to CUI.
  3. Check it is not a sales pitch and that you can obtain release approval for your materials.
  4. Submit through the official form by December 4, 2026.
