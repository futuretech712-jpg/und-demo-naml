Directions for days 3-4 are below. Days 3-4 are on a Navy installation. NIWC Pacific's general visitor guidance asks official visitors to request access well in advance (at least 15 business days). Confirm the workshop's access process with the organizers at naml@us.navy.mil.

Getting to NIWC Pacific

Days 3-4, April 14-15. Restricted sessions for appropriately cleared attendees.

  1. No CAC or DBIDS yet? Start at the Visitor Center

    Pick up your DBIDS credential here. This is also the taxi and rideshare drop-off and pick-up point.

    NIWC Pacific Visitor Center
    49275 Electron Dr, San Diego, CA 92152
    Visitor Center in Google Maps

  2. Enter through the Mills St. Gate

    You must have a CAC or DBIDS credential to enter.

    Mills St. Gate
    Mills St, San Diego, CA 92106
    Mills St. Gate in Google Maps

  3. Park and walk to the session

    Parking is along Electron Dr. The map shows the driving route (green) and the walking route (orange) to the A33 Main Entrance and Bldg. 99.
