About the workshop

NAML is a forum for interdisciplinary experts from government, industry, and academia to discuss ongoing work on the theory and applications of machine learning to address some of the most challenging problems faced by our Naval forces and their partners.

Call for participation

Submit a short abstract of 2,000 characters or less for a short talk (20 minutes with Q&A), a lightning talk (5 minutes), a poster, or another format such as a panel or special session. Presentation formats are subject to change.

Abstracts must be unclassified and may contain up to Controlled Unclassified Information. They are not shared outside the government review team. Abstracts that read like a sales pitch will not be considered; companies can take part through sponsorship.

Do not submit classified information into the form.

Questions

Can my abstract contain CUI?

Yes, up to Controlled Unclassified Information. Never submit classified information.

I work for a company. Can I present?

Yes, as long as the abstract is not a sales pitch or a promotion of a product or service. For company visibility, contact RaChelle Curato (racurato@deloitte.com) about sponsorship.

When will I hear back?

Authors will be notified by mid-February 2027.
