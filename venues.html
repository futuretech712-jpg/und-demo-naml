NAML 2027 · San Diego, California

Venues

The workshop runs over four days at two venues. The first two days are open to all participants; the last two are for appropriately cleared attendees.

Days 1–2 · April 12–13

Hyatt Regency La Jolla

The first and second days of the workshop are held at the Hyatt Regency in La Jolla and are open to all participants.

Access: open to all registered participants.

Open in Google Maps
Days 3–4 · April 14–15

NIWC Pacific

The third and fourth days are held at the Naval Information Warfare Center Pacific.

Access: only appropriately cleared attendees who have a CAC or similar government ID, or are able to obtain a DBIDS credential.

Open in Google Maps

Registration fee

There will be a registration fee (TBD) to attend NAML 2027. Workshop registration will open soon.

The fee is waived for government civilian employees and military personnel.

Presenting?

Presenting authors are responsible for obtaining the approvals needed to release their presentation materials before the workshop.
